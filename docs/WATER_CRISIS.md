# Water Crisis Intelligence

## Goal

Detect, analyze, and visualize water stress, drought conditions, and long-term rainfall deficiencies using geospatial data, climate datasets, satellite imagery, and AI-assisted environmental analysis.

The system should help users understand:

* areas facing water scarcity
* regions with prolonged low rainfall
* groundwater stress zones
* drought-prone regions
* environmental risk patterns

---

# Core Features

## Rainfall Deficiency Monitoring

Track:

* current rainfall
* historical rainfall averages
* monsoon deviations
* multi-year rainfall trends

Examples:

* "Rainfall is 40% below historical average."
* "Region has experienced below-normal rainfall for 3 consecutive years."
